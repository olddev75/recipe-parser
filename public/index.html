        How to Use Recipe Deck

        Simple guide to importing, scaling, converting, and cooking recipes.

        1. Importing Recipes

        You can add recipes in three easy ways using the left sidebar tabs:

        • Text: Paste raw recipe text or captions.
        • Web URL: Enter food blog links to scrape ingredients & steps automatically.
        • Photo: Upload cookbook photos or screenshots for AI OCR extraction.

        2. Scaling Portions & Unit Conversion

        Use the top toolbar buttons to scale servings up or down (+/-) or switch between Metric (grams/milliliters) and Imperial (ounces/cups) measurements instantly.

        3. Cook Mode & Kitchen Timers

        Click 🍳 Cook Mode for a big-screen step runner with Screen Wake Lock. Click inline timer buttons (e.g. ⏱️ 15 mins) to start kitchen countdown chimes!
