      How to Use Recipe Deck

      Easy, step-by-step guide to help you cook and save recipes

      📝

      1. How to Add a Recipe

      You can add any recipe in three simple ways:

      • Paste Text: Copy recipe text or tap 📋 Paste, then tap ✨ Format & Parse Text.
      • Web Link: Paste any food blog or recipe URL into 🔗 Web URL and tap 🌐 Scrape & Import Recipe.
      • Upload a Photo: Take a picture or screenshot on your phone, drop it into the box, and tap 🔍 OCR & Extract Recipe.
      ⚖️

      2. Change Serving Sizes & Units

      • Change Servings: Tap the - and + buttons. All ingredient amounts calculate automatically for your group.
      • Switch Units: Tap Metric for grams and milliliters, or Imperial for ounces and cups.
      🍳

      3. Interactive Cook Mode & Timers

      • Step-by-Step View: Tap Cook Mode for big, high-contrast instructions that are easy to read across the kitchen counter.
      • Screen Stays On: The app keeps your phone or computer screen awake while you cook so it won't fall asleep.
      • Instant Kitchen Timers: Whenever a recipe step says "cook for 15 minutes", a timer button appears. Tap it to start a live countdown.
      ⭐

      4. Save, Rate, and Organize

      • Save to Library: Tap Save Recipe (or Update Recipe) to store recipes permanently.
      • Star Ratings: Tap 1 to 5 stars to keep track of your favorite meals.
      • Difficulty Badge: Tap the difficulty pill to toggle between 🟢 Easy, 🟡 Medium, and 🔴 Hard.
      • Tag Filter Pills: Click protein or cuisine pills (like Chicken, Pasta, Thai) in the sidebar to filter your collection with one tap.
      🌐

      5. Preferred Language

      Choose your preferred language from the dropdown menu in the top header at any time.
